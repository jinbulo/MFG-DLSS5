[size=6][b]SkyrimSE MFG — Real Multi-Frame Generation + DLSS 5 Neural Rendering for Skyrim Special Edition[/b][/size]

[size=5][b]Description[/b][/size]

Skyrim Special Edition has neither DLSS nor frame generation. This mod adds both, on top of your existing [b]ENB[/b] setup, without touching it.

It takes the window over with its own D3D12 flip swapchain and drives NVIDIA Streamline / DLSS-G itself, so the game gets [b]genuine multi-frame generation[/b] (2x / 3x / 4x / 5x / 6x) even though the engine knows nothing about DLSS. The in-between frames are produced by the driver's frame generator, not smeared out by a post-process filter.

Upscaling and DLSS 5 neural rendering come from [b]dlss5-feed[/b] + [b]renodx-dlss5 8.5[/b], and this mod feeds them the [b]engine's own TAA motion vectors[/b] — the same R16G16_FLOAT buffer Skyrim uses for its own TAA, published straight into ReShade's MOTION_VECTOR by [b]FO4ReShadeHelper[/b] — instead of optical flow. It also hands over the game's real depth buffer (converted to R32_FLOAT) and the engine's own camera pair, so neither the upscaler nor the frame generator has to guess anything. In practice: motion reads exactly [b]0 px while you stand still[/b] and tens of px while you turn, with no outliers.

The UI is handled properly too: [b]ui_exact[/b] lifts the HUD, compass and subtitles out before DLAA runs and composites them back afterwards, so they keep their original pixels while the world still gets DLAA. No mask, no threshold, no edge bleed — any HUD, any resolution, translucency included.

Nothing in your game folder is modified permanently: the installer backs up everything it overwrites, ENB is never touched, and the layer turns off with one double-click.

[b]Two builds have been tested end to end — these are the configurations we can vouch for:[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Skyrim SE 1.5.97[/b] — SKSE64 [b]2.0.20[/b] + ENB [b]0.505[/b]. This is the longest-validated baseline (takeover, 4x, ui_exact, and both motion-vector paths).
[*][b]Skyrim SE 1.7.104 (AE)[/b] — SKSE64 [b]2.3.1[/b] + ENB [b]0.505[/b]. Verified: takeover OK, 4x (21 → 85 fps on screen), motion vectors 100% non-zero (mean |mv| 7.56 px). On AE the motion vectors come from the engine buffer via ENB; this mod's own camera-matrix fallback path stays 1.5.97-calibrated and does not engage there.
[/list]

[b]Running a different build?[/b] The two above are what has been tested end to end. Everything else is untested rather than blocked: install the ENB and the SKSE64 that match your build, keep the window borderless, keep other frame-generation layers off, and try it. If something misbehaves, post the four logs and we can tell you exactly where it stands.

If you want the exact configuration that has been validated the longest, run 1.5.97 — the archive ships [b]DOWNGRADE-to-1.5.97.md[/b] with the complete Steam console recipe if Steam has already put you on AE.

[size=5][b]Main features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Real frame generation, 1x–6x.[/b] MFG_FRAMES selects 2x / 3x / 4x / 5x / 6x (1 = 2x, 2 = 3x, 3 = 4x … 5 = 6x). Verified in game: ~90–108 fps on screen against ~23–27 fps application-side at 2560x1600 (4x).
[*][b]Real motion vectors — no optical flow.[/b] The engine's TAA motion buffer (R16G16_FLOAT) is published into ReShade's MOTION_VECTOR by FO4RESHADEHELPER and handed to DLSS-G and to the neural renderer. One writer, constant units, no double-peak, no ghosting guesswork.
[*][b]Real depth and real camera.[/b] The game's own depth buffer is converted to R32_FLOAT; the camera pair comes from the engine's previous-frame world matrix, not from an estimated pose.
[*][b]DLSS 5 neural rendering[/b] through [b]renodx-dlss5 8.5[/b], wired so its compute-state shadow lands on the right command-list world (WAIT_DLSS_FEED=1 in SKYRIMMFG.INI). Do not turn that off.
[*][b]The UI stays pixel-exact (UI_EXACT, on by default).[/b] HUD, compass and subtitles are lifted out before DLAA and composited back, so they keep their original pixels while the world still gets DLAA. Turn it off with UI_EXACT=0 if you want to compare.
[*][b]Verified end to end:[/b] in a full session the whole path ran on 100% of frames (UIEXACT | 累计: 帧 4183，有A 4183，改写 4183，合成 4183), MFG reported 4x actual = 4x requested, NR-VERDICT STATE=ENGAGED RATIO=0.9993, and the log held zero errors.
[*][b]Two numbers that must match your game.[/b] The camera model uses a horizontal FOV of 77° (CAM_FOV_DEG=77) and Skyrim's default near plane of 15 units. If you changed your FOV, set CAM_FOV_DEG to your actual horizontal FOV — the log prints an FOV 判据 line that helps you pin it down from an in-place turn frame. If frame generation looks "off" or motion smears, check this first.
[*][b]Configurable HUD handling.[/b] UI_EXACT and UI_EXACT_RTV control the exact-UI path; HUDLESS_TAG accepts only 0/1 (anything ≥2 is clamped back to 1 — the 2/3 path was measured twice, made things worse, and has been removed from the code). The HUD [i]inside generated frames[/i] is interpolated by DLSS-G itself — that part is by design.
[*][b]One-line off, separate log, on-screen frame counter[/b] (STATS_WINDOW=1, ENABLED=0 for a passive layer without renaming anything).
[*][b]Safe by construction.[/b] Nothing in DATA\ is touched, ENB's D3D11.DLL / ENBSERIES.INI / ENBLOCAL.INI are neither shipped nor overwritten, and every install is preceded by an automatic backup.
[/list]

[*][b]Mod manager —[/b] optional. MO2 (copy into OVERWRITE\ROOT) or no manager at all (run INSTALL.BAT).
[/list]

[b]Performance.[/b] Neural rendering is the one big cost: about [b]24 ms per frame, 38–45% of the frame[/b]. If your application-side frame rate drops below ~15 fps, either lower MFG_FRAMES to 2 (3x) or set NRRESOLUTIONSCALE=0.5 in RESHADE.INI (that takes neural rendering down to roughly 7 ms). On an 8 GB card the full setup also sits near the VRAM limit at 2560x1600. Remember that the on-screen counter counts [i]displayed[/i] frames including generated ones — input latency follows the lower, application-side number in the log.

[*][b]整链实测：[/b]一局里 100% 的帧跑完整条链（UIEXACT | 累计: 帧 4183，有A 4183，改写 4183，合成 4183），实测倍数 = 自报倍数 = 4×，NR-VERDICT STATE=ENGAGED RATIO=0.9993，日志零错误。
[*][b]两个必须与你游戏一致的数字。[/b]相机模型按水平 FOV 77°（CAM_FOV_DEG=77）与默认近平面 15 单位工作。改过视野就把 CAM_FOV_DEG 改成实际水平 FOV —— 日志里的 FOV 判据 行能帮你从「原地转身」的帧里反推。插帧发糊时先查这个值。
[*][b]HUD 相关开关。[/b]UI_EXACT / UI_EXACT_RTV 控制精确 UI 通路；HUDLESS_TAG 只接受 0/1（写 ≥2 会被钳回 1，2/3 那条路实测两次都是负收益，代码已删）。生成帧里的 HUD 会被 DLSS-G 自己插值 —— 那是设计如此。
[*][b]一行停用、独立日志、屏上帧数读数[/b]（STATS_WINDOW=1；ENABLED=0 让整层被动而不改文件名）。
[*][b]安全：[/b]不动 DATA\，不附带也不覆盖 ENB 的 D3D11.DLL / ENBSERIES.INI / ENBLOCAL.INI，安装前一定先备份。

[b]性能。[/b]唯一的大开销是神经渲染：[b]约 24 ms/帧，占整帧 38–45%[/b]。应用侧掉到约 15 fps 以下时，把 MFG_FRAMES 降到 2（3×），或在 RESHADE.INI 里把 NRRESOLUTIONSCALE 设成 0.5（神经渲染降到约 7 ms）。8 GB 显存的卡在 2560×1600 下会贴近显存上限。屏上数字是[b]显示帧数[/b]（含生成帧），手感/延迟看日志里较低的那个应用侧数字。
